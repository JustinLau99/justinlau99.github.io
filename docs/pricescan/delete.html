PriceScan — Request that your data is deleted

App: PriceScan (com.justindev.pricescan)
Developer: Justin Lau
Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page explains how to delete data related to PriceScan on Google Play. We do not operate a PriceScan account system or a backend that stores your camera photos or scan history.

How to request deletion

  1. Email justinlau99dev@gmail.com from an address we can reply to.
  2. Use the subject line: PriceScan data deletion request.
  3. Include the Google account email you use on the device and, if you can, your device model and Android version.

We will respond within 30 days and explain what you can remove on your device, what we do not store, and what only Google or other providers can address.

Data you can delete on your device (immediate)

Most PriceScan data stays only on your phone or tablet until you remove it:

  • Scan history and saved scan images
  • App settings, theme, language, and onboarding state
  • Cached exchange rates and local “Remove ads” entitlement flag

Steps:

  • Use any in-app options to clear history, if available.
  • Android: Settings → Apps → PriceScan → Storage → Clear data (or Clear storage), or uninstall PriceScan.

Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local PriceScan data we control on that device. We do not keep a copy on our servers to delete.

When you email us

We will confirm that we do not hold a PriceScan user profile, scan photos, or scan history on developer-operated servers, and we will point you to the steps below for Google ads, Play purchases, and exchange-rate requests.

Google ads (AdMob) — we do not store this; Google holds it

When the free version shows ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may collect data such as advertising ID, ad interactions, and technical data (for example IP-based approximate location). We do not receive or store those ad logs on our servers, and we cannot delete Google’s ad data on your behalf.

What you can do (Google):

  • Android: limit or reset your advertising ID and ad personalization in Settings → Google → Ads (wording may vary by device).
  • Use Google’s privacy and ad controls, for example My Ad Center and Google’s Privacy Policy.

What we can do in the app only:

  • If you purchased Remove ads, banner ads in PriceScan are turned off; that does not erase historical records Google may already hold.
  • Uninstalling PriceScan stops this app from requesting further ads; past processing by Google is still governed by Google’s policies and your Google settings.

Google Play purchases

Remove ads purchase records are kept by Google Play. We only store a local flag on your device that you own the entitlement. Clear app data or uninstall to remove that local flag. Purchase history deletion or export is handled through Google Play, not through us.

Exchange rates (Frankfurter)

PriceScan requests currency rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). Those requests may include currency codes and technical data such as IP address on their servers. We do not send photos or OCR text. Retention and deletion for that provider follow Frankfurter’s policies, not ours.

Retention summary

  • Local app data: on your device until you clear app data or uninstall; we do not operate a matching server copy.
  • AdMob / Google ads data: held and retained by Google and partners; manage via Google settings and tools above.
  • Play purchases: retained by Google Play under Google’s policies.
  • Frankfurter logs: retained by Frankfurter under their policies.
