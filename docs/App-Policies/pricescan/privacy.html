PriceScan Privacy Policy

App: PriceScan (com.justindev.pricescan)
Effective date: 5 November 2026
Last updated: 5 November 2026

This privacy policy describes how PriceScan (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application on Android and iOS. By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Summary

  • Camera images used for text recognition (OCR) are processed on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers.
  • Exchange-rate requests go to a public rate provider and include currency codes (and standard network data such as your IP address on their side)—not your camera images.
  • Optional scan history and app settings are stored locally on your device until you delete them.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect data for advertising as described below.
  • You can buy a one-time “Remove ads” purchase through Google Play or the App Store; payment is handled entirely by the store, not by us.

Information we process

Camera and on-device OCR

PriceScan uses your device camera to read prices and text from labels and menus. Frames you capture are processed on-device for optical character recognition (OCR). That processing does not send your images to servers operated by us. You control camera access through your device settings; the app cannot scan without permission you grant.

Scan history (on your device)

If you use scan history, the app may save entries locally on your device, including cropped images and related conversion details (such as amounts and currency hints). This data stays on your device unless you clear history in the app or uninstall the app. We do not receive a copy of your scan history.

Exchange rates

To show live conversions, the app requests exchange rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). Requests include the currency pair being queried (for example USD/EUR). Frankfurter’s servers may log technical data such as IP address and request metadata under their own policies. We do not send photos or OCR text to Frankfurter as part of rate lookups.

App preferences and cache

Settings such as theme, language, currency board layout, cached exchange rates, onboarding state, and whether you purchased “Remove ads” are stored locally using your device’s storage (for example SharedPreferences on Android). Clearing app data in system settings removes this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When ads are shown, we use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google and its partners may collect and use information such as device identifiers, coarse location (if available to the SDK), app activity related to ads, and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where allowed by your settings and applicable law.

You can limit ad personalization on your device (for example, “Opt out of Ads Personalization” on Android or “Limit Ad Tracking” on iOS). If you purchase Remove ads, banner advertisements in the app are turned off; the SDK may still initialize as part of the app, but ad units are not displayed while your entitlement is active.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchases

The optional non-consumable product Remove ads (product ID: com.justindev.pricescan.lifetime_no_ads) is sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store your payment card details. Purchase and restore flows are handled by the store; we only store a local flag that you own the entitlement so the app can hide ads.

Reviews and external links

If you choose “Leave a review”, the app may open the store’s review flow. Optional support links (for example, a “Buy me a coffee” page) open in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not require you to create an account in PriceScan.
  • We do not operate our own backend that receives your camera photos for OCR.
  • We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where GDPR applies, we process data to provide the app (contract / legitimate interest), to show ads in the free version (consent or legitimate interest depending on ad type and your choices), and to comply with legal obligations. You may have rights to access, delete, or restrict processing of personal data held by third parties such as Google; contact them using the links above. For data stored only on your device, you can delete it via the app or by clearing app storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party ad or store flow, contact us and we will help you address it with the relevant provider.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy. For material changes, we may also show a notice in the app.
